How to Create a Facebook Page & Get Access Token
Required to auto-post to your Facebook Page
# Step What to do Where
1 Create Facebook Page Go to facebook.com → click the ⊞ Menu → select Page → click Create new Page → enter your business name and category facebook.com/pages/create →
2 Go to Developers Open Facebook for Developers → click My Apps → click Create App → choose Business type developers.facebook.com →
3 Add Facebook Login In your app dashboard → click Add Product → find Facebook Login → click Set Up → choose Web Inside your new app
4 Get Page Access Token Go to Graph API Explorer → select your app → click Generate Access Token → select your Page → grant pages_manage_posts and pages_read_engagement permissions Graph API Explorer →
5 Get Long-Lived Token In the Explorer → run: GET /me/accounts → copy the access_token from your Page — this is your permanent Page token Graph API Explorer
6 Connect in Autopostleey Go to Connections → click Connect Facebook → log in with your Facebook account → select your Page → done! Autopostleey Dashboard
💡 Tip: The easiest way is to just click Connect Facebook in Autopostleey Connections — it handles the OAuth automatically. You only need the manual steps above if the automatic connection doesn't work.
How to Create an Instagram Business Account
Required to auto-post to Instagram — personal accounts cannot be automated
# Step What to do Where
1 Open Instagram App Open the Instagram app on your phone → go to your Profile (bottom right) → tap the ☰ Menu (top right) Instagram mobile app
2 Go to Settings Tap Settings and privacy → scroll down to find Account type and tools → tap it Instagram Settings
3 Switch to Professional Tap Switch to professional account → tap Continue → choose a category that best describes your business → tap Done Account type and tools
4 Choose Business Select Business (not Creator) → tap Next → add your contact info (email, phone) → tap Next Professional setup
5 Link to Facebook Page When prompted, tap Connect a Facebook Page → select your Facebook Page → tap Done. If you skip this, go back to Settings → Linked accounts → Facebook During setup
6 Verify Business Account Your profile will now show a Contact button and Category label. You should see Professional dashboard in your profile menu — this confirms you have a Business account Your Instagram profile
7 Connect in Autopostleey Go to Connections → click Connect Instagram → log in with Facebook → select your Instagram Business account → allow permissions → done! Autopostleey Dashboard
⚠️ Important: Instagram requires your account to be Business or Creator type AND linked to a Facebook Page before Autopostleey can post automatically.
✅ Personal accounts: Use Manual Posting in Connections — AI writes your caption and copies it, then opens Instagram for you to paste.
